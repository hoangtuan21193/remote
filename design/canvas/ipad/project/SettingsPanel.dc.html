Three fingers on the trackpad or over the stream. Left or right steps through your apps here: stop and the one lit opens. The others go to Mac Studio: while a window streams, a gesture shows the Desktop first, and the opposite gesture closes what one opened. Mac Studio does these with its own keyboard shortcuts: one turned off in its Keyboard settings does nothing. Four-finger swipes and a Magic Keyboard trackpad’s gestures stay with iPadOS: change Spaces with Mission Control.
