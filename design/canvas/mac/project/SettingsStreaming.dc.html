Sill streams at these while every connected device is away from home, through a VPN or over the internet, so a slow connection never starts at your home quality. A device away from home changes these, and never your Quality and Resolution above.

Prioritize encoding speed

The hardware encoder is busy or not answering, so streams use the software encoder: up to 60 fps at Standard resolution. Sill switches back by itself once it keeps up again.
